# ANTI-AI-SLOP RULES

This document constrains all future human or AI edits.

## Do not introduce

- generic purple/blue SaaS gradients
- glassmorphism card grids
- large collections of rounded cards
- meaningless glowing orbs
- AI-generated stock illustrations
- fake testimonials
- fake customer logos
- fake metrics
- unsupported claims
- emoji feature lists
- generic “revolutionize / empower / unlock / next generation” copy
- repetitive feature sections without a real information need
- decorative animations that compete with reading
- excessive shadows and border-radius
- duplicated sections produced only to make the page longer

## Required behavior

- every section must have a clear reason to exist
- every claim must be supportable
- prefer real service names, prices and scope to vague benefits
- typography and spacing should do more work than decorative components
- preserve accessibility and responsive behavior
- images need a defined brand role

## Review question

> Would this still feel specific to Sintara if the logo were removed?

If the answer is no, revise it.
